description: Composable charts with animation, tooltips, and themes.
---

The charts are part of [Cortexcn Pro](/pro). Every chart has a live preview and full docs here, and the source comes with Pro. Mix lines, areas, and time-aligned columns on one chart with the [**Composed Chart**](/docs/charts/composed-chart) (`SeriesBar` + `Line` / `Area`).

The parts every chart shares, such as the legend, grid, tooltip, brush, and axes, are documented under [Chart Utilities](/docs/utility/legend). To see every chart with live examples, open the [chart gallery](/charts/area-chart).
